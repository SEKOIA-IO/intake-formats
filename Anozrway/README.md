# Anozr Way

## Description

Anozr Way monitors the exposure of the individuals of an organization: it detects the data leaks that expose
their professional identity and computes a risk score per individual. At the end of each scan, the
Anozr Way platform pushes an alert to Sekoia.io over HTTPS for every new professional leak exposing
an individual and for every individual whose risk score becomes high.

## Intakes

- [Anozr Way](anozrway): the new professional leaks of an individual and the individuals reaching a high risk
